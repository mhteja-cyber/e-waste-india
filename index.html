UNDERSTANDING THE PROBLEM

What is E-Waste?

Electronic waste is becoming an important environmental and resource-management issue as the use of electronic devices continues to increase.

E-waste refers to discarded electrical and electronic equipment and their components. Examples include mobile phones, computers, televisions, printers, refrigerators and many other electronic products.

Electronics can contain both valuable materials and substances that require careful management. When electronic waste is handled improperly, resources can be lost and environmental risks can increase.

Responsible management includes reducing unnecessary consumption, extending product life through repair and reuse, and ensuring discarded electronics enter appropriate collection and recycling systems.

INDIA'S REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

Government & E-Waste Policy

India has established a regulatory framework for managing electronic waste and defining responsibilities across the product lifecycle.

📜

E-Waste Management Rules, 2022

India's E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 provide the regulatory framework for covered electronic waste management and came into force on 1 April 2023.

🏭

Producer Responsibility

The framework establishes responsibilities for relevant stakeholders involved in the electronic product lifecycle.

♻️

Recycling Framework

The rules provide a framework for collection, recycling and environmentally sound management of covered e-waste.
